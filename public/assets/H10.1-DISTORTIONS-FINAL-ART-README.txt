FINAL EVENT RULES
- 5 Distortion events per week.
- Exactly one of each of the five Distortions is scheduled each week.
- Five distinct days are chosen randomly each week.
- Weekday starts: randomly between 5:00 PM and 8:59 PM America/Denver.
- Weekend starts: randomly between 12:00 PM and 8:59 PM America/Denver.
- Each Distortion lasts 3 hours.
- Hunt cooldown becomes 30 minutes while active.
- All hunters are released to hunt immediately at opening.
- 10 minutes before collapse, all hunters receive one final Hunt reset exactly once.
- Normal hunt cooldown rules return after collapse.
- World Shatter remains disabled.
- Ultra Hunts remain disabled.
- Existing reward engine, comeback points/bonuses, Hunter Points, Hunt Tokens, companion XP, pet abilities, inventory/egg rewards, catch history and leaderboards remain underneath the Distortion layer.

H10.1 CHANGE
Removed the old relic-discovery prerequisite from automatic Distortion scheduling. These are now independent recurring season events.
